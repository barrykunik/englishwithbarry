# AI Teaching & Implementation Business Launch Plan

> **Goal:** Launch a two-tier AI services business (teaching + implementation) by September 2026, with first teaching cohort and implementation projects closed by December 2026.

**Architecture:** Two parallel workstreams (May-August prep) converging into integrated launch (Sept-Dec):
1. **Teaching Preparation** — Curriculum finalization, course platform setup, marketing materials
2. **Implementation Setup** — Website modifications, case studies, pricing/proposal templates

**Timeline:** 
- **May-August:** Preparation and setup (4 months)
- **September:** Teaching launch
- **Oct-December:** First implementations

## Notes for Implementation

**Mindset:**
- This is an iterative business. You'll learn and adjust as you go.
- First cohort won't be perfect — that's fine. Get real feedback and improve.
- Implementations get easier as you do more. First one will be the slowest.
- Teaching is your strength. Let that guide everything.

**Key Reminders:**
- Commit work to git regularly (don't wait until August)
- Keep curriculum materials in version control
- Document everything (case studies, testimonials, learnings)
- Stay in touch with school contacts — they're your best lead source
- Start simple implementations (2-3 hour projects), work up to complex ones

**If You Get Stuck:**
- Curriculum clarity: Ask early students for feedback
- Implementation complexity: Partner with a specialist or break into smaller phases
- Website: Use a page builder (Webflow, Framer, or simple WordPress) if coding feels overwhelming
- Time management: September-December is execution season. This plan is May-August prep. You have time.
