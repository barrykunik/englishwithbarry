## Executive Summary

Launch a two-tier AI services business leveraging 20 years of teaching expertise:
- **Tier 1 (Teaching):** Teach individuals and organizations how to use AI tools effectively
- **Tier 2 (Implementation):** Implement AI workflows and process automation for clients discovered through teaching

The teaching offering serves as the entry point and discovery mechanism for implementation projects, creating a natural upsell funnel and long-term client relationships.

**Timeline:** May-August preparation, September 2026 launch, scaling through end of year.

## Business Model

### Core Concept: Teaching → Implementation Pipeline

Teaching is not a standalone product; it's the entry point to deeper client relationships. Here's the flow:

1. **Client enrolls in teaching** (course, workshop, coaching)
2. **Through practical lessons**, you understand their business context and pain points
3. **Client recognizes** problems that could be solved with AI automation
4. **You propose implementation** ("I can automate X workflow for you")
5. **You implement the solution**, train them, deliver value
6. **Ongoing relationship** for optimization and new automations

### Why This Works

- **Trust-building:** Teaching establishes credibility; 20 years of teaching experience differentiates you
- **Market insight:** Hands-on teaching reveals real client needs (not assumptions)
- **Natural upsell:** Implementation isn't pushy; it's a logical next step for problems you've discussed
- **High-value engagements:** Implementation projects generate meaningful revenue while solving real problems
- **Sticky customers:** Clients who learned from you are more likely to hire you for custom work

## Tier 1: Teaching Offering

### Target Audiences

**B2B - Educational Institutions:**
- English schools and language centers
- Entry point: Warm leads (schools already expressed interest)
- Audience: Teachers and students learning AI
- Delivery: Group workshops, structured courses, in-person or online

**B2C - Individual Professionals:**
- Professionals aged 50-60+ wanting to understand AI
- Audience: Career changers, retirees, lifelong learners
- Delivery: One-on-one coaching, small workshops, flexible scheduling

### Curriculum (~30 Hours)

**Structure:** 10 core lessons covering tools, techniques, and real-world application

**Module 1: Foundations**
- What is AI and why it matters
- How AI tools work conceptually (without technical jargon)
- Overview of different AI categories (text, image, voice, etc.)

**Module 2: Core Tools Deep Dive**
- ChatGPT: Features, use cases, limitations
- Claude: Capabilities, when to use it vs. ChatGPT
- Gemini: Integration, Google ecosystem advantages
- Other specialized tools: DALL-E 3, Perplexity, Canva Magic, etc.

**Module 3: Practical Skills**
- Prompting fundamentals (how to ask AI the right questions)
- Prompt engineering (optimization, iteration, testing)
- Tool chaining (using outputs from one tool as input to another)
- Workflow automation basics

**Module 4: Real-World Application**
- Case studies: How businesses use AI to solve problems
- Identifying automation opportunities in their own work
- Building custom workflows for their context

**Module 5: Hands-On Projects**
- Guided projects using real tools
- Practice with their own data/use cases
- Troubleshooting and iteration

### Delivery Formats

**For Schools:**
- Workshops (2-4 hours per session, 1-2 sessions per week)
- Structured courses (ongoing engagement, 8-10 weeks)
- Teacher training + student training (parallel tracks)
- Pricing: Per-session or per-course bundles

**For Individuals:**
- One-on-one coaching (weekly or bi-weekly sessions)
- Small group workshops (4-6 people)
- Self-paced modules with optional Q&A sessions
- Pricing: Hourly rate or fixed workshop price

### Success Metrics (Teaching)

- First cohort completes curriculum by December 2026
- At least 2-3 school partnerships active by Q4 2026
- Positive feedback and completion rates >80%
- Clients expressing interest in automation/implementation

## Tier 2: Implementation Offering

### Scope: What You Implement

**AI Workflow Implementation:**
- Mapping client's business processes
- Identifying automation opportunities
- Building workflows using AI tools (ChatGPT, Claude, Zapier, Make, etc.)
- Integrating AI into existing systems
- Testing and refinement

**Process Automation:**
- Repetitive task automation (email, data entry, scheduling, etc.)
- Document processing and analysis
- Content generation pipelines
- Customer service automation
- Knowledge base creation

### Typical Project Flow

**Phase 1: Discovery & Proposal (2-3 weeks)**
- Understand client's workflow and pain points
- Identify specific automation opportunities
- Create proposal with scope, timeline, and pricing

**Phase 2: Implementation (4-8 weeks)**
- Build and test AI workflows
- Integrate with client's existing tools
- Create documentation and runbooks
- Iterative refinement based on feedback

**Phase 3: Training & Handoff (1-2 weeks)**
- Train client team on new workflows
- Document processes for ongoing maintenance
- Establish support/optimization plan

**Phase 4: Ongoing Support (optional)**
- Monthly check-ins
- Optimization of workflows based on usage
- Integration of new tools/features
- Scaling existing automations

### Pricing Strategy

**Project-Based Pricing (Recommended):**
- Small automation: €2,000-5,000 (simple single workflow)
- Medium workflow: €5,000-15,000 (multi-step, multiple tools)
- Enterprise solution: €15,000+ (complex, multi-department)

**Alternatively: Hourly Consulting**
- €75-150/hour for consultation and implementation
- Scope estimated upfront, billed hourly within agreed range

**Ongoing Support:**
- €500-2,000/month for maintenance and optimization

## Key Assumptions & Risks

### Assumptions
1. Schools will follow through on interest and enroll teachers/students
2. Automation opportunities will emerge naturally from teaching interactions
3. Clients will be willing to invest in implementation ($2K-15K projects)
4. Summer 2026 prep time is sufficient to launch in September
5. Your growing AI knowledge will be sufficient for initial implementations

### Risks & Mitigation

**Risk:** Slow teaching enrollment
- *Mitigation:* Direct outreach to school contacts, offer free intro workshop, leverage social proof

**Risk:** Difficulty identifying implementation opportunities
- *Mitigation:* Ask students directly, conduct needs assessment in later curriculum modules, offer free consultation

**Risk:** Implementation complexity exceeds your knowledge
- *Mitigation:* Start with simpler automations, partner with specialists if needed, continue learning throughout

**Risk:** Pricing too high or too low
- *Mitigation:* Offer free consultation first, iterate pricing after first projects, use hourly as fallback
